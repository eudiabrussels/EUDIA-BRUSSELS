Our Team

The coordinators who lead our projects, academic partnerships and digital work.

Fatma Ünlü

Project Director

Responsible for planning, coordinating and delivering EUDIA’s projects. Manages cooperation between the internal team and external partners throughout the implementation of joint projects.

Ahmet Emirhan Abay

Coordinator for Universities & Academic Partnerships

Leads relations with universities, academics and research institutions. Contributes to developing academic partnerships, joint studies and educational programmes for young people.

Ekrem Karatmanli

Coordinator for Digital Technologies & Artificial Intelligence

Coordinates EUDIA’s digital infrastructure, website and social media work. Develops projects for the effective use of artificial intelligence and digital technologies in the organization’s activities.
