About Us

EUDIA Brussels is the institutional identity of the European Diaspora Studies Organization, based in Belgium.

About EUDIA

A meeting point for diaspora communities, institutions and partners.

EUDIA Brussels is a Belgium-based organization that brings together diaspora communities, institutions and partners from different countries. Its activities focus on international cooperation, youth and education, culture and society, professional networks and community engagement.

Diaspora work is EUDIA’s central and priority field. EUDIA seeks to maintain direct contact with diaspora communities, strengthen connections between organizations, develop joint projects and contribute to sustainable relationships across countries.

Based in Brussels, EUDIA is open to cooperation with diplomatic missions, public institutions, European institutions, universities, civil-society organizations, businesses and diaspora organizations.

Bringing people, institutions and diaspora communities together across borders.
Mission & Vision

Why we exist and where we are heading

Mission

To strengthen communication and cooperation among diaspora communities; create new connections between young people, institutions, professionals and different parts of society; and develop lasting relationships through international partnerships, events, visits, projects and knowledge sharing.

Vision

To become a trusted, active and inclusive organization in the diaspora field by building a strong network of communication and cooperation extending from Brussels across Europe and internationally.

Our Approach

EUDIA is not limited to research. It is an active organization that engages with communities, connects institutions, develops international relations, organizes activities and supports the creation of joint projects.

Diaspora at the Core

Diaspora is at the center of our institutional identity.

Our work considers not only challenges faced by diaspora communities, but also their human capital, cultural heritage, younger generations, entrepreneurial capacity and international networks.

EUDIA values stronger communication among diaspora organizations, greater participation of younger generations and direct cooperation with institutions in different countries.

Cooperation with Türkiye

Türkiye is an important field of cooperation for EUDIA because of its strong social and institutional ties with diaspora communities in Europe. EUDIA is open to developing joint events, visits, youth activities, cultural programs and international projects with public institutions, universities, local authorities, civil-society organizations, businesses and other relevant organizations in Türkiye.

Institutional Identity

The meaning of our logo

The three-bar E The three-bar E in the EUDIA wordmark represents the three founders.
Six stars The six-star system is a fixed part of EUDIA’s institutional symbolism reflecting its connection with Turkic states and the diaspora. The star above the I is included in the total of six stars.
