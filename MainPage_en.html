About EUDIA

Diaspora work is our central and priority field.

EUDIA seeks to maintain direct contact with diaspora communities, strengthen connections between organizations, develop joint projects and contribute to sustainable relationships across countries.

Based in Brussels, EUDIA is open to cooperation with diplomatic missions, public institutions, European institutions, universities, civil-society organizations, businesses and diaspora organizations.

Diaspora at the Core

Diaspora is at the center of our identity.

Our work considers not only the challenges faced by diaspora communities, but also their human capital, cultural heritage, younger generations, entrepreneurial capacity and international networks.

Cooperation with Türkiye

Dialogue and cooperation between Europe and Türkiye.

EUDIA is open to developing joint events, visits, youth activities, cultural programs and international projects with public institutions, universities, local authorities, civil-society organizations and businesses in Türkiye.
