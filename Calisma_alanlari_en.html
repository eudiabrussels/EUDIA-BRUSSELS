Our Areas of Activity

EUDIA’s activities are organized around eight main fields.

01

Diaspora Activities

Communication and cooperation with diaspora communities, joint projects and stronger links between communities.

02

International Relations & Cooperation

Relations with institutions in different countries, international projects and joint activities.

03

Interinstitutional & Diplomatic Relations

Engagement and cooperation with embassies, consulates, public bodies, European institutions, universities and other organizations.

04

Youth & Education

Activities supporting education, careers, international experience and social participation of young people.

05

Culture & Society

Preserving cultural heritage, encouraging intercultural encounters and strengthening relations between communities.

06

Economy, Entrepreneurship & Professional Networks

Connecting businesspeople, entrepreneurs and professionals and supporting international cooperation.

07

Events & International Meetings

Conferences, forums, visits, meetings, study visits and activities bringing participants from different countries together.

08

Research & Knowledge Sharing

Research, information activities, publications and knowledge sharing related to diaspora and EUDIA’s fields of activity.

EUDIA is not limited to research; it is an active organization that engages directly with communities.
