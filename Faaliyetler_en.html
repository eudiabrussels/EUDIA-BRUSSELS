Institutional Visits

We meet public institutions, think tanks and academic circles to build new areas of cooperation between Europe and Türkiye.

Brussels, Belgium

Visit to the Permanent Delegation of Türkiye to the EU

A EUDIA Brussels delegation led by our Founding President Sevda Bağator met Deputy Permanent Representative Mr. Orhan Aydın and EU Counsellors Mr. Arda Deniz Aksular and Ms. Aysel Tolunay at the Permanent Delegation of Türkiye to the European Union.

We discussed Türkiye–EU relations, the diaspora, the international representation of young people, academic cooperation and opportunities for joint work.

We thank them for their kind hospitality and valuable insights.

Ankara, Türkiye

Visit to the Directorate for EU Affairs, Ministry of Foreign Affairs

As part of our institutional meetings in Ankara, EUDIA Brussels visited Ms. Elif Kurşunlu, Director General for Relations with the European Union at the Directorate for EU Affairs of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Türkiye.

We discussed EUDIA Brussels’ Brussels-based international vision, our diaspora work in Europe and the projects we aim to develop in youth, academia, diplomacy and international cooperation.

We also met Mr. Bülent Özcan, Director General for Financial Cooperation and Project Implementation, and exchanged views on EU funds, project development processes and opportunities for international partnerships.

For us, these meetings are more than institutional visits. They are an important part of building new areas of cooperation between Europe and Türkiye based on knowledge, experience and shared goals, and of making the experience of our communities in Europe more visible at academic, diplomatic and institutional levels.

Visit to Prof. Dr. Hakan Karataş, Vice President of Defence Industries

A EUDIA Brussels delegation paid a visit to Prof. Dr. Hakan Karataş, Vice President of Defence Industries of the Republic of Türkiye, and held a productive meeting.

We shared detailed information on EUDIA Brussels’ founding vision, its activities across Europe, its institutional goals and its projects for the coming period.

We discussed the importance of bringing Türkiye’s national technology and capability vision in the defence industry to young generations, universities and academic circles in Europe.

In particular, we explored opportunities for joint work on connecting young people living in Europe with Türkiye’s defence industry ecosystem, developing academic cooperation between universities, supporting young engineers, and increasing knowledge and experience sharing in artificial intelligence and advanced technologies.

Ankara, Türkiye

Meeting with Dr. Mustafa Arslan in Ankara

EUDIA Brussels met Dr. Mustafa Arslan, researcher at the Türkiye Research Foundation, in Ankara.

We held in-depth discussions on the current situation of the Turkish diaspora in Europe, migration policies, diaspora diplomacy, Türkiye–Europe relations and opportunities for international academic cooperation.

Our meeting with Dr. Mustafa Arslan, faculty member at Ankara Medipol University and Founding Director of the Ankara Diaspora and Diplomacy Research Centre, provided a valuable exchange of ideas for EUDIA Brussels’ work in diaspora research and academic cooperation.

We exchanged views on strengthening academic ties between Europe and Türkiye, developing joint research areas and building sustainable cooperation in diaspora studies.

Ankara, Türkiye

EUDIA Brussels Visits SETA

EUDIA Brussels executives, including our Secretary General Murat Aslan, met Assoc. Prof. Dr. Murat Aslan, researcher at SETA.

The meeting offered a comprehensive exchange of views on current developments, international relations and perspectives for strategic cooperation.

We thank Assoc. Prof. Dr. Murat Aslan for his kind hospitality and valuable insights.
